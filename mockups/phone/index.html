Celeste for Android: mock

A clickable mock for review. Nothing here talks to a real computer or phone.

On your phone: run npm run mock:phone and scan the QR it prints (same Wi-Fi).

Here: the frame is the Galaxy S24's size (360 × 780). The DEMO pill or D jumps to each scenario; Esc is Android's Back.

Greyish Android screens (installer, App info, Settings) are Android's own, drawn only to show the steps.
